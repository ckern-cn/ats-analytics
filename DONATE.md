# Donate (optional)

ATS is free software. All functionality is in the open-source code; there is no
paid tier, feature gate, license key or time limit.

Donations are **voluntary**. They do not buy:

- extra features or earlier access,
- priority support or a response-time guarantee,
- roadmap influence or a vote,
- the right to use the official brand in a fork.

If you do not want to donate, that is completely fine. Using the project,
starring it, reporting bugs, improving docs, translating the UI or testing a
release are all valuable support.

- Donations are not tax advice and may not be tax-deductible.
- A donation is not a purchase of a service and does not create a commercial
